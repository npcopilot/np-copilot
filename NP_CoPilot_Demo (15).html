Accuracy depends on data entered — verify before relying on results
The Care Gap Navigator reads only from data entered in this visit form. Until EHR integration is live, results are only as complete as what you have manually entered.
Before running the analysis, confirm:
  • Active Dx tab — all current diagnoses entered with ICD-10 codes (required for diabetic eye exam, CKD monitoring, lung LDCT, and other condition-specific rules)
  • Preventive Care tab — prior screenings entered with dates and status (determines overdue vs. up to date)
  • Social History tab — smoking status and pack-years entered (required for lung LDCT and AAA screening rules)
  • Patient demographics — age and sex entered in the Consent tab (required for all age- and sex-specific rules)
Results reflect the data available at the time of analysis. After EHR FHIR integration, the navigator will pull this data automatically before every visit.
